# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering peers evaluating Ahmed Hesham Lotfy's software development experience.

## Product Purpose

An interactive digital CV that makes Ahmed's training, projects, education, and technical range easy to scan while preserving a clean print-ready resume.

## Positioning

The CV presents career evidence as a compact engineering system: dense enough for technical review, but with interactive details that reward exploration without hiding the core facts.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from professional profiles, review the page on mobile or desktop, and may print or download a PDF for an application workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The surface includes typed CV content, project and experience navigation, status telemetry, lightweight diagnostic easter eggs, and print parity. It must not invent facts beyond the supplied CV payload.

## Brand Commitments

The visual direction is monochrome, precise, and industrial: deep obsidian, zinc borders, crisp foreground, and restrained motion. Inter Variable is the primary typeface.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical CV payload supplied in the build brief. No additional testimonials, metrics, or imagery are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make factual evidence scannable before making the interface expressive.
- Keep interaction optional; the printed document must stand alone.
- Use motion to explain state, never to decorate content.
- Treat technical density as a design material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and print-specific styling.
